Caldo Verde

Description

Caldo verde is one of my favorite Portuguese recipes. This creamy soup made with linguiça sausage and potatoes is greenish in color thanks to a whole pound of fresh kale. Enjoy it with a thick slice of your favorite bread.

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 medium onion, minced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 6 medium potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 2 quarts cold water
  • 6 ounces linguiça sausage, thinly sliced
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • Ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 pound kale, rinsed and julienned
